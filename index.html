Why this is different

Most bots are sold on a P&L screenshot.
Mine are delivered with a ledger.

A screenshot can't tell you whether the profit is real, or if it's luck, stale prices or a bug quietly ignoring positions. The only way to know is to measure every fill against the exchange and kill the ideas that don't hold up.

The usual "trading bot"

  • Backtest on prices you could never have traded
  • Reads the public REST book, seconds behind the market
  • P&L from its own marks, never checked against the exchange
  • One kill switch, if any, that resets on restart
  • Black box you rent; the seller keeps the code

Method

Real money is the last gate, not the first

Every strategy has to pass each gate on measured data. Most ideas die at gate 2 or 3, which is far cheaper than finding out live.

gate 1

Scope

30-minute call, then a written scope: venue, strategy, risk limits, price.

exit: signed scope
gate 2

Record

Capture the live book and every trade on your markets. No assumptions about the data.

exit: clean data
gate 3

Replay

Run the strategy against the recording with queue position and reaction delay.

exit: markout ≥ 0
gate 4

Paper

Live market, simulated fills, the same code that will trade.

exit: replay ≈ paper
gate 5

Small live

Small size behind hard caps. The ledger has to match the exchange before size goes up.

exit: Δ $0.00

Edge check

Does your idea survive the fee?

Many "edges" are smaller than the cost of trading them. Put in your number.

Edge per share (fair − price)
Taker fee per share
Adverse selection per share
Net per share
Net per trade

Taker fee uses Polymarket US's published formula, 0.0695 × p × (1 − p) per contract (about 1.7¢ at 50¢); resting (maker) orders pay none. "Adverse selection" is how far price tends to move against you right after a fill: in my sports research it was about 0.5–2¢ within two minutes. Illustrative only; not trading advice.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Will you trade my money or sell me signals?

No. You hold the account and the API keys; the system trades your strategy within the limits you set. I build it, hand it over, and can keep it running, but I don't manage money, take a share of profits, or sell trade signals.

Can you guarantee it will make money?

No one honestly can. What I guarantee is measurement: you'll know from the ledger and markouts whether it does, and the Edge Study exists so you can find out before paying for a full build.

Which venues do you support?

Polymarket US and Kalshi for event contracts, and major crypto exchanges with REST and WebSocket APIs. If the venue has a documented API, it's usually buildable; we confirm in scoping.

Who owns the code?

You do. The repository, deployment scripts and runbooks are transferred on delivery.

How long does a build take?

A Starter Bot is typically 2–3 weeks to live. A custom build is typically 4–8 weeks from deposit to small-live, depending on venue and strategy complexity. Paper trading time is part of the schedule, not an extra.

Do I need a strategy already?

For a build, yes, at least a clear idea of how you'd price the market. If you're not sure there's an edge, start with an Edge Study.
